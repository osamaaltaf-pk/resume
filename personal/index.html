02 Research

Three questions I want to spend years on

Building products showed me where my understanding runs out. Each of these started as a real failure in something I shipped.

01

LLMs and VLMs that can show their work

When I fine-tuned Llama 3.2 Vision on radiography, it wrote fluent, report-like text almost immediately. Proving that the text came from a specific region of the image was much harder. I want to understand grounding, not just fluency.

  • VLMs
  • Grounding
  • Medical imaging

02

Many agents, many users

Wisko runs a chain of agents for many users at once, and most failures live in the handoffs: shared state, memory, and who is allowed to see what. I want to study multi-agent systems and multi-user LLMs as a research problem, not only an engineering one.

  • Multi-agent
  • Memory
  • Multi-tenant LLMs

03

Inference, and learning while serving

Most of my working hours have gone into making models fast enough to use: KV caching, quantization, batching, sub-second voice pipelines. I want to go deeper into efficient inference and into how models reason and adapt at inference time.

  • KV cache
  • Quantization
  • Test-time compute
